# Visual QA matrix

[Русская версия](../ru/visual-qa-matrix.md)

Test real Bot API output, not screenshots generated by the renderer. Record client version, OS, theme, font scale, chat type, and adapter route with every result.

| Surface | Light | Dark | Large text | Narrow screen | Reply/topic | Forwarded | Expected result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Plain short answer | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | No unnecessary heading, card, or broken entity |
| Heading and divider | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Hierarchy remains obvious without relying on color |
| Inline accents and links | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Link targets work; underline is not ambiguous |
| Details and anchors | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Summary stays visible; anchors land on the intended row |
| Two-column table | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Values remain readable without clipped prose |
| Rowspan or colspan table | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Spans remain coherent and screen-reader order is sensible |
| Code and formula | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Long tokens wrap or scroll safely; no space-based layout |
| Photo with caption/credit | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Credit is visible and spoiler state is intentional |
| Collage | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Simultaneous comparison is legible |
| Slideshow | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Frame order is clear and swipe carries sequence |
| Nested media details | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Supported clients render it; fallback moves media out |
| Map | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Coordinate context remains readable; aspect ratio is stable |
| Custom emoji | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Alternative text is meaningful if the glyph is unavailable |
| Buttons | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Buttons sit outside the card and perform a real action |
| Draft streaming | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | topic | n/a | Draft expires; final persistent message appears once |
| Legacy fallback | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | not tested | Core answer, warnings, captions, and order survive feature loss |

Minimum clients: current Telegram Android, iOS, and Desktop. Re-run the matrix after Bot API renderer changes or a client regression report.
